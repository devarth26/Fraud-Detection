# POL-003 Synthetic Identity and New-Account Abuse

Policy ID: POL-003
Applies to rules: NEW_CARD_HIGH_RISK, NEW_CARD_ELEVATED, CRYPTO_NEWCARD_INTERNATIONAL
Applies to personas: SYNTHETIC_IDENTITY

## Definition
Synthetic identity fraud uses a fabricated identity, often combining real and invented details. The account builds little or no history and then makes a large purchase quickly, with no behavioural baseline for models to compare against.

## Detection thresholds
- A card under 6 months old with no prior transaction history and a first transaction above 2,000.
- A new card used immediately for crypto purchases or international transfers.

## Required actions
1. Hold the transaction and request enhanced due diligence documents for the account holder.
2. Re-verify identity against independent sources, not only the documents supplied at onboarding.
3. Review other accounts sharing the same address, phone number, device or email for linked identities.
4. If verification fails, close the account and recover any exposure through the loss recovery team.

## Documentation
List the verification steps attempted, the documents reviewed and any linked accounts found.
