# POL-010 Escalation, Human Review, Reporting and Documentation

Policy ID: POL-010
Applies to rules: all
Applies to personas: all

## Human review
- Every case rated HIGH risk, or with a weighted score of 0.75 or above, must be reviewed by a human analyst before the card is blocked permanently or a report is filed. Automated systems may place a temporary hold only.
- The analyst records one of three decisions: approve the recommended action, escalate, or dismiss as a false positive. A written reason is mandatory.

## Service levels
- HIGH risk: analyst response within 1 hour.
- MEDIUM risk: response within 8 hours.
- LOW risk: reviewed in the next daily batch.

## Escalation
- Escalate to the fraud operations lead if the customer cannot be reached, if losses exceed the delegated authority, or if multiple linked accounts are found.
- Escalate to compliance when money laundering or terrorist financing is suspected.

## Regulatory reporting
Where laws require it, a suspicious transaction or activity report is filed with the relevant authority (for example an STR with FIU-IND in India or a SAR in the US). The decision to file belongs to the compliance officer, not the fraud analyst, and the customer must not be told.

## Documentation and retention
Every case note records the transaction, the signals observed, the policy applied, the action taken, the analyst name and the time. Case records are retained for at least 5 years.
