# CASE-002 Account takeover with VPN and new device

Case ID: CASE-002
Related policies: POL-002, POL-007
Outcome: confirmed fraud

## Summary
A long-standing card with a 60 average transaction suddenly made a 3,100 purchase from a high-risk country on a new device using a VPN, at 2am. The customer could not be reached for 3 hours. The analyst froze outgoing transfers, forced a credential reset and escalated to the fraud operations lead under POL-002.

## Lesson
The deviation from the customer's own baseline mattered more than the absolute amount.
